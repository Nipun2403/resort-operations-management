# Brag Plan: AETHERIS

## What is this app?
Aetheris is an ultra-luxury private estate management platform — a full-stack hotel operating system covering public bookings, six staff operation portals, an AI concierge, and real-time operations.

## The angle
Behind every serene luxury experience is an invisible command center. The video opens with the polished public face of Aetheris — the silent peak of luxury — then pulls back to reveal the operational engine: six mission-control portals (Admin, Front Desk, Kitchen, Housekeeping, Maintenance) plus the guest's own Private Corridor, all orchestrating the stay. The contrast between surface serenity and operational depth is the story.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-screen dark void (#131411). Gold serif line fades in: "Behind every silent peak…" — pause — "…is a command center." Typography is Playfair Display in #e4c285.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The face of Aetheris** — "The Silent Peak of Luxury" hero headline with the glassmorphism booking bar (ARRIVAL / DEPARTURE / GUESTS)
- **The guest journey** — "The Private Corridor" guest portal: booking confirmation, room service menu grid, Atlas AI concierge chat
- **Mission Control** — Five operations portals revealed as labeled command center screens: ADMIN (metrics dashboard), FRONT DESK (check-in), KITCHEN (order queue), HOUSEKEEPING (task board), MAINTENANCE (requests)

## Outro / punchline
AETHERIS wordmark in gold (#e4c285), full-screen. Tagline: "Private Estate & Refuge. Command Center Included."

## User flow worth showing
Guest browses rooms on the public site → books a stay → views booking in The Private Corridor guest portal → operational portals handle the rest. This is the centerpiece: the serene guest flow (scenes 2-3) contrasted with the operations engine (scene 4).

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic with mission-control energy
- Creative direction: "Behind the velvet rope — luxury surface meets operational depth"
- Interpretation: The guest-facing scenes (1-3) are slow, polished, elegant — restrained reveals with Playfair Display serif typography, slow crossfades. Scene 4 (Mission Control) shifts to faster cuts, harder transitions, and more energetic pacing. The outro returns to elegance.

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm cinematic bed with energy ramp into the mission-control sequence
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: fade in at 0s at volume 0.30. Gentle swell toward scene 4 (mission control). Fade under the final logo at ~17s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset available at assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md (110 BPM). Strong cue targets: 8.74s (0.99, scene 3 transition), 13.11s (0.98, mission control entrance), 17.47s (0.99, outro transition). Beat grid available for sequential portal reveals (~0.54s spacing at 110 BPM — snap to every other beat to keep text readable).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make hero glow and card presence breathe. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate — soft reveal sounds for guest scenes, crisp interface sounds for mission-control portal reveals
- Audio-coupled moments: scene 4 — each portal label slams in with a sequenced card/casing sound; final logo gets a single deep bell hit
- Restraint rule: no aggressive or chaotic sounds — even the mission-control sequence should feel controlled and premium

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 0–3s
Full-screen black (#131411). Text fades in centered:
"Behind every silent peak…" (Playfair Display, gold #e4c285, 48px)
1.5s pause.
"…is a command center." fades in below.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines appear one after another, each with a soft fade-in
Audio intent: music bed enters low (0.15), builds slightly on second line
Audio-coupled idea: none — let the music bed carry the weight
Music: fade in at 0s, volume 0.15→0.30 by end of scene
Transition mood: slow crossfade (0.6s) → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Face — 3–7s
Hero screen: "THE SILENT PEAK OF LUXURY" in large Playfair Display gold. Subtitle "PRIVATE ESTATE & REFUGE" in Manrope caps (#e4e2dd, labeled, smaller). Below, the glassmorphism booking bar resolves in: three card panels (ARRIVAL / DEPARTURE / GUESTS) sliding in from left, then "RESERVE SANCTUARY" CTA button appears.
Sequential/interaction: yes — subtitle appears, then booking cards slide in left to right, one by one
Audio intent: warm, cinematic, establishing
Audio-coupled idea: each booking card lands with a soft card-place or drop SFX
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Guest Journey — 7–11s
Transition to "THE PRIVATE CORRIDOR" label (Manrope caps, gold accent). Three panels appear:
1. Booking confirmation card with details
2. Room service menu grid (3 menu items)
3. Atlas AI concierge chat bubble
Each appears as a glassmorphism card, staggered.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards appear one by one with staggered timing
Audio intent: elegant, premium, calm
Audio-coupled idea: card-slide-1.ogg SFX per card arrival; last card gets a soft success accent
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Mission Control — 11–17s
Hard cut to faster pace. Full-screen dark with a grid layout. Five labeled "command center" cards slide in, one per beat:
1. ADMIN — metrics: occupancy 94%, ADR $1,280, RevPAR $892
2. FRONT DESK — check-in workflow, active bookings
3. KITCHEN — order queue, prep status
4. HOUSEKEEPING — room cleaning task board
5. MAINTENANCE — maintenance request list
Each card: dark surface container (#1f201d), golden label at top, Manrope body text. Cards appear sequentially left→right, top→bottom.
Sequential/interaction: yes — five cards appear one by one; text inside each card is pre-rendered (no staggered line reveals within cards to avoid reading-time issues)
Audio intent: energetic, building, controlled urgency
Audio-coupled idea: each portal card arrives with an interface switch or click sound; final card (MAINTENANCE) gets a stronger impact to transition to outro
Music: volume holds at 0.30; energy from quicker cuts
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 17–20s
Full-screen dark. "AETHERIS" wordmark appears center, gold Playfair Display large (72px, letter-spacing -0.02em). Below it, tagline in Manrope: "Private Estate & Refuge. Command Center Included." (#e4e2dd, 18px, letter-spacing 0.02em). Everything holds for 2s. Fade to black at 20s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — AETHERIS wordmark appears first with a glow-up, tagline fades in 0.5s later
Audio intent: final, resolved, definitive
Audio-coupled idea: deep resonant bell (impactBell_heavy_000.ogg) hits as AETHERIS lands; music fades out over final 2s
Transition mood: fade to black at 20s

**Music mood for this video:** warm cinematic → building energy → resolved finale
**Audio summary:** A warm, steady cinematic bed builds gently through the guest experience, tightens into a controlled rhythmic pulse for the mission-control sequence, then resolves into a single deep bell hit as the logo lands.
