Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our highest principle.

Effective Date: July 2, 2026

Introduction

At Aetheris, discretion is not a courtesy — it is our highest law. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and limit access to the personal information of our guests, prospective guests, and website visitors. By engaging with Aetheris, whether through inquiry, reservation, or residence, you entrust us with a degree of privacy we do not take lightly.

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide a seamless, secure, and personalized experience:

  • ✦ Identity information (full name, government-issued ID where legally required for stay registration)
  • ✦ Contact information (email, phone, mailing address)
  • ✦ Payment and billing information, processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant channels
  • ✦ Stay preferences (room selection, dietary requirements, amenity requests, special occasions)
  • ✦ Referral information, where a guest has been introduced by an existing member of the Aetheris circle
  • ✦ Security and access records necessary to maintain the safety of the property and its guests

We do not collect information beyond what is required to serve you. We do not run public loyalty programs, marketing pixels, or third-party ad trackers on our reservation systems.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

Management

Process and manage reservations seamlessly.

Personalization

Personalize your stay, dining, and amenity experience.

Communication

Communicate essential stay-related information.

Exclusivity

Maintain the security and exclusivity of the property.

We do not sell, rent, or trade guest information under any circumstance.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

Guest information is shared only where strictly necessary — for example, with payment processors, or with private transport and security partners engaged on your behalf, and only under binding confidentiality agreements. Aetheris does not disclose guest identities, stay dates, or preferences to press, media, or public inquiry, regardless of the requesting party, except where compelled by law.

Data Security

All guest data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is restricted on a strict need-to-know basis to a limited number of senior staff, each bound by a lifetime confidentiality agreement. Physical records, where they exist, are held in restricted-access, climate-controlled archives.

Data Retention

Guest information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above, or as required by law, after which it is securely and permanently destroyed.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Requests may be made through our private inquiry channel below and will be honored within a reasonable timeframe, consistent with our confidentiality obligations to other guests.

Children's Privacy

Aetheris is an adults-oriented retreat. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18 without an accompanying guardian's authorization.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in law or practice. Material changes will be communicated directly to guests with active reservations.

Privacy Inquiries

Should you require clarification on our principles, please contact our Office of Discretion for private consultation.
